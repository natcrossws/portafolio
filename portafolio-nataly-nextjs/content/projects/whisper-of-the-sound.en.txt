Whisper of the Sound
--------------------
Generative art with neural networks: digital painting driven by live instrumental sound.

In progress · Listening lab

The idea
--------
A live performance—piano, voice, or percussion—generates textures that accumulate, deform, and dissolve. The image remembers what it heard, and silence empties it.

I am not aiming for the piece to identify instruments with precision, but to listen to behavior: energy, attacks, tonality, noise. That is the difference between a work that listens and a visualizer that only reacts to volume.

Where the project stands
------------------------
The project moves lesson by lesson, each one documented. The first is already working: a listening lab in the browser that captures the microphone and shows in real time what arrives.

• Capture without videocall processing, so harmonics are not clipped.
• Waveform, logarithmic-scale spectrum, and a level meter with peak.
• Pitch and note detection (C1 to C7), with deviation in cents.
• Real-time classification among voice, tonal sound, percussion, and noise.
• Instrument recognition with an AI model (AST/AudioSet) running in the browser.
• First seeds of memory: peaks that decay and notes that sustain.

In parallel I am working the visual line: a StyleGAN2 model trained on paint textures.

What comes next
---------------
1. Listen by bands: lows, mids, and highs as their own signals, plus attack detection.
2. Distinguish music from noise and read the behavior of sound.
3. First generative canvas: particles, Perlin noise, screen persistence, and shaders.
4. Controls to test live how each sound translates into image.

The map of the work: lows → dense motion · mids → fluid · highs → particles · amplitude → deformation · rhythm → pulses · silence → dissolution.

Technologies
------------
JavaScript · Web Audio API · Transformers.js · AST/AudioSet · StyleGAN2 · p5.js (coming soon)
