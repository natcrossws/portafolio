Lore
----
A digital library with game-like logic.

In progress · Design phase

The idea
--------
What if reading felt like leveling up in a video game? Lore is a digital library where reading progress becomes visible: each book counts, an avatar represents you, and a literary pet keeps you company.

Where the project stands
------------------------
Before writing code, I designed the foundations of the system:

• Architecture: how the application is organized and how its parts connect.
• Database: the model for books, reading progress, avatars, and pets.
• User experience: the flows and the experience of use.

What comes next
---------------
• Visual prototype of the first screens.
• Start of development on the architecture and data model already defined.
