Development of a digital platform for Mexico’s Ministry of Labor and Social Welfare (STPS), aimed at modernizing the intake, management, and follow-up of reports related to possible labor-rights violations, workplace accidents, and contractual breaches.

The platform integrates differentiated interaction flows by report type, forms for information capture, and mechanisms to keep each request moving. In this way, citizen-service processes become a structured digital experience that makes both filing and follow-up easier.

The project is an application of technology in public service: software development responds to concrete operational needs and helps improve accessibility, organization, and traceability of institutional processes.
