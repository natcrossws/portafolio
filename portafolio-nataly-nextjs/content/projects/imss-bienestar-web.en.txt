Development of the IMSS-Bienestar national web portal, an institutional platform built to centralize and present information about the organization’s services, programs, and activities.

I took part in building the site end to end: the public interfaces and, centrally, the high-impact editorial sections—news and other urgent publishing channels—where the institution communicates sensitive, time-critical information. For those sections I designed and implemented a purpose-built CMS so content could be published quickly, clearly, and under control, without manual page edits.

The project had to serve both the people who browse the portal and the teams who keep published information up to date.

Web interface development
-------------------------
Implementation of the portal’s visual structure and components, organizing institutional content so it is easy to browse and stays coherent across the site.

Content management logic
------------------------
Implementation of a CMS focused on news and urgent content sections: create, edit, and publish flows designed so editorial teams can release critical information on time, with a clear structure and without touching the portal’s code.

Interface and administration integration
----------------------------------------
Work connecting the public presentation of content with the administration logic, considering both the browsing experience and day-to-day maintenance of institutional information.
